## 1. Registry Investigation: Why `cosm.dev` Was Unavailable

During initial DNS checks, `cosm.dev` returned `NXDOMAIN` (no active DNS nameservers), creating the illusion of availability. However, querying **Google Registry RDAP** (`https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/cosm.dev`) directly confirmed:
- **Registry Status**: `['client transfer prohibited']` (Registered / Parked).
- **Outcome**: `cosm.dev` is already owned and cannot be purchased on Cloudflare or standard registrars without private acquisition.

All domains listed in this document have been **authoritatively verified directly against registry endpoints**:
- **Google Registry RDAP** (for `.dev` and `.app`)
- **Verisign WHOIS / RDAP** (for `.com` and `.net`)
- **ICANN Authoritative WHOIS** (for `.io`, `.sh`, `.tools`)

* **Core Unit of Currency**: **`cosm`** (Micro-universe / parallel dimension).
* **CLI Command**: **`topo`** or **`cosm`**.
* **Acronym (T.O.P.O.)**: **T**arget **O**rchestration & **P**olyglot **O**bjects.

* **Core Metaphor**: Polyphonic Music & Verse Structure. *Strophic form* ($A-A-A$) is the fundamental musical structure where different lyrics/verses are sung over an identical underlying harmonic melody. Concurrent AI agent swarms generate distinct verses of code that follow identical underlying AST contract rules and compile into a unified song.
* **Core Unit of Currency**: **`verse`** (or `strophe` / parallel track).
* **CLI Command**: **`verse`** or **`stroph`**.
* **Acronym (V.E.R.S.E.)**: **V**irtual **E**nvironment, **R**outing & **S**emantic **E**ngine.
* **SEO Profile**: **8.5 / 10** — Generic dictionary entry in music/poetry, but **zero software or tech collisions** on GitHub, PyPI, or package registries. Instant #1 for developer-intent queries (`strophic cli`, `strophic scm`, `strophic.dev`).
* **Cloudflare Domains Verified Available**:

  - `stroph.dev` (Matches 6-letter CLI)
  - `strophic.io`
  - `strophic.sh`
  - `stroph.io`
  - `stroph.sh`
